Break-Glass Admin Access Procedure

RESTRICTED: visible only to members of the it-admins group. This fictional document exists to demonstrate that ACL filtering keeps restricted chunks out of anonymous results.

When to use break-glass access

Break-glass accounts are used only when single sign-on is unavailable and a SEV1 incident requires administrative access to the identity provider.

Procedure

Two members of it-admins must approve the request in the access workflow. The approved engineer retrieves the sealed break-glass credential from the hardware-backed vault; retrieval triggers an alert to the security team.

After use

The credential is rotated within 4 hours of use and the session recording is attached to the incident record for review.
