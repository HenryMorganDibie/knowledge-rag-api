Incident Response Runbook

This runbook defines how production incidents are declared, escalated and closed. Anyone may declare an incident; it is always better to declare and downgrade than to wait.

Severity levels

SeverityDefinitionResponse timeUpdate cadence
SEV1Customer-facing outage or data exposure15 minutes, 24x7Every 30 minutes
SEV2Major feature degraded for many customers1 hour, 24x7Every 2 hours
SEV3Minor degradation with a workaroundNext business dayDaily

Roles

The incident commander coordinates the response and owns decisions. The communications lead posts status page updates at the cadence for the severity. Subject matter experts investigate and report findings to the incident commander.

Escalation

Page the on-call engineer through the paging system. If there is no acknowledgement within 10 minutes, the page escalates automatically to the secondary on-call, and after 20 minutes to the engineering manager.

Post-incident review

Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident requires a blameless post-incident review within 5 business days. The review records the timeline, root cause, and action items with named owners and due dates.
