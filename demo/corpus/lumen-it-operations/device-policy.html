Device and Encryption Policy

This policy applies to every laptop and mobile device used for Lumen work.

Encryption

Full-disk encryption must be enabled on all laptops. Device management verifies encryption daily and blocks access to company systems from unencrypted devices.

Patching

Operating system security updates must be installed within 14 days of release, and within 72 hours for updates rated critical. Devices that fall behind are restricted to the Company Portal until they are updated.

Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device to the Service Desk within 24 hours, or immediately if it may contain customer data. IT will issue a remote wipe and revoke the device's certificates and sessions. Filing a police report is recommended for theft.

Screen lock

Devices must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity and require a password, PIN or biometric to unlock.
