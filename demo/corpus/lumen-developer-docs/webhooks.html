Webhooks

Webhooks notify your service when documents change in Lumen Cloud. Register an HTTPS endpoint and choose the events to subscribe to, such as document.created, document.updated and document.deleted.

Verifying signatures

Each delivery includes an X-Lumen-Signature header containing an HMAC-SHA256 digest of the raw request body, computed with your endpoint's signing secret. Recompute the digest over the exact bytes you received and compare it with a constant-time comparison. Reject the delivery if the signatures differ.

The X-Lumen-Timestamp header records when the delivery was signed. Reject deliveries whose timestamp is more than 5 minutes old to prevent replay attacks.

Retries and delivery guarantees

Deliveries are at-least-once. Respond with any 2xx status within 10 seconds to acknowledge a delivery. Failed deliveries are retried up to 5 times with exponential backoff over 24 hours, after which the event is marked as failed and visible in the delivery log. Use the event id to make your handler idempotent.

Rotating the signing secret

Rotating a signing secret keeps the previous secret valid for 24 hours, so your endpoint can verify signatures from both secrets during the rollover.
