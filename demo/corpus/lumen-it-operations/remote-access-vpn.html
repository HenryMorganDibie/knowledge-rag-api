Remote Access VPN Setup

Staff working outside a Lumen office must connect through the corporate VPN to reach internal systems such as the build farm and the finance portal.

Installing the client

Install the VPN client from the Company Portal app on a managed laptop. Personal devices are not permitted to connect. After installation, sign in with your corporate account.

Multi-factor authentication

Every VPN connection requires multi-factor authentication through the authenticator app. Push approvals expire after 60 seconds. If you lose your phone, contact the Service Desk to re-register a device before connecting.

Split tunnelling

Only traffic to internal address ranges goes through the VPN. Video calls and general internet traffic use your local connection, which keeps meetings responsive.

Troubleshooting

If the client reports a certificate error, the laptop has probably not checked in with device management for more than 30 days. Connect to an office network or run a manual sync from the Company Portal, then retry. Persistent failures should be reported to the Service Desk with the client log attached.
